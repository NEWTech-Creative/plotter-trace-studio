Gestures & shortcuts

Touch / Apple Pencil
One finger or Pencil draws · Two fingers — pan & pinch zoom
Two-finger tap — undo · Double-tap (Select/Pan tool) — fit page
Palm is ignored while the Pencil is down · “Finger drawing” can be turned off in Stroke to make one finger pan instead

Keyboard
V select · B draw · P pen · L line · N nodes · E erase · H pan
⌘Z undo · ⇧⌘Z redo · ⌘S save · Delete removes selection
Esc cancels the current tool action · +/− zoom · 0 fit page
